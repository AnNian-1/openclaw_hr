## 岗位职责

1. Own APAC feature roadmap for B2B SaaS modules.
2. Gather customer insights from regional sales & CS; prioritize backlog.
3. Write PRDs in English; align with global product council.
4. Measure adoption KPIs and run quarterly business reviews.

## 任职要求

**硬性要求**
- 4+ years PM experience in B2B SaaS or enterprise software.
- Fluent English for writing and meetings.
- Strong stakeholder management across cultures.

**加分项**
- Experience with global product launches.
- SQL / analytics literacy.
