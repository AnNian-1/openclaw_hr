## 岗位职责

1. Design and develop backend services for global SaaS platform (Java / Kotlin / Go).
2. Own service reliability (SLO 99.95%) and participate in on-call rotation (约每月 1 周)。
3. Collaborate with global PMs and UX in English; write design docs in English.
4. Improve CI/CD, testing, and observability practices.
5. Mentor junior engineers through code review.

## 任职要求

**硬性要求**
- Bachelor+ in CS or related; 3-7 years backend experience.
- Strong in distributed systems, APIs, relational & NoSQL DBs.
- Professional working proficiency in English (书面 + 会议).
- Experience with cloud (主流公有云之一（比赛表述）).

**加分项**
- Experience in multi-region systems.
- Open source contributions.
- Familiar with compliance (SOC2 / GDPR) engineering practices.

**软素质**
- Inclusive communication, ownership, continuous learning.
